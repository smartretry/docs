---
title: Update future transaction
description: Override the amount or reschedule a specific upcoming payment within a recurring series.
openapi: "/api-reference/openapi.json PUT /v1/payments/recurring/future-transactions/update/{terminal_friendly_id}"
---

Update one future transaction using its `future_transaction_id` and `recurring_id`. Supply an
optional amount, scheduled date, or both. Use
[recurring update](/api-reference/recurring/update) to update the recurring series instead.

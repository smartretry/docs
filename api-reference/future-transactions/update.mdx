---
title: Update future transaction
description: Override the amount or reschedule a specific upcoming payment within a recurring series.
openapi: "/api-reference/openapi.json PUT /v1/payments/recurring/future-transactions/update/{terminal_friendly_id}"
---

Updates a single future transaction within a recurring series. You can override the charge amount for that specific occurrence, reschedule it to a different date, or both. Changes apply only to the targeted transaction - the rest of the series schedule is unaffected.

<Note>
  To change the default amount or schedule for all future payments in a series, use the [recurring update endpoint](/api-reference/recurring/update) instead.
</Note>
